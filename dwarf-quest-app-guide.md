# Dwarf Quest — Campaign Tracker Guide

This app tracks the whole DQ27 campaign — the map, renown, your war band, the shared Barracks, and the run-up to the Arrival of the Gods finale. Everyone uses the **same link**, on their own device. It updates automatically every few seconds, so you don't need to refresh constantly — but if something looks stale, a manual refresh never hurts.

Open it, bookmark it, and you're set for the whole weekend.

## First time opening it

You'll land on a "Who goes there?" screen. Pick one:

- **Command Console** — for any of the GMs. You'll be asked which GM you are (just so we can tell who's driving on which device) — type your name and go in.
- **Your name** — under your team's colour, tap yourself from the player list.

That choice **sticks to this device** — you won't see this screen again unless you tap **Switch** in the top-right corner. So:

- Use the **same device** all weekend if you can — it remembers who you are.
- If you hand your phone to someone else, or grab someone else's, tap **Switch** first so you don't end up editing the wrong person's war band.
- If your name isn't listed yet, ask a GM to add you in Roster.

## If you're a GM

Your console has six tabs:

- **Map** — the district map. Tap a district to assign it to a team, set its renown value, and edit its control benefit. The Warband Advances panel and Finale god-tier setup live below the map too.
- **Turn** — the current turn's scenario title and intro text. Edit it, move between turns, and hit **Tally & Advance Turn** to bank renown for whoever controls what.
- **Battles** — once both teams have submitted their deployment plans, reveal the matchups here, tap the winner of each game, and award Baraz-Klink.
- **Balance** — a live comparison of both teams' strength, plus expandable "Top Warriors" and "Top Killers" leaderboards. Use this mid-event if one side's pulling ahead and a scenario needs balancing.
- **Roster** — add/rename players, edit team names and colours, manage the Armoury catalog (weapons/armour/skills), the Special Rules catalog, and the shared Barracks (mercenaries).
- **Ledger** — renown totals and history, plus manual +/- adjustment buttons for on-the-spot rulings.

**Any GM can use any device** — there's no split of powers between you three, it's just whoever's got a screen open.

## If you're a player

Your console has five tabs:

- **Scenario** — read the current turn's intro, and see your team's finale progress (Legend/Mortal counts, god slots) as it develops.
- **War Band** — build and manage your own fighters. Tap **Add blank member** or **Add from template** to create one, then tap any member to open their card: name, Quality/Combat, three loadout slots, Special Rules, kill count, feats, and (once live play starts) an Out of Action toggle and a Found Items log.
- **Armoury** — spend your Baraz-Klink on weapons, armour, and skills. Buying something adds it to your inventory; you still need to equip it onto a specific war band member from their card.
- **Barracks** — your team's *shared* pool of hired mercenaries. Recruit one (costs Baraz-Klink), and anyone on your team can then **Claim** it into their own war band for a game, or **Return** it when they're done. If a mercenary shows "with a teammate," it's just unavailable until they release it.
- **Deploy** — each turn, assign your teammates to that round's games and add private notes. Hit **Submit** when your team's ready. Once both teams submit, a GM reveals the matchups.

**A few things worth knowing:**

- **Quality is backwards from what you'd expect** — lower is better (2+ is elite, 6+ is poor), matching Song of Blades and Heroes. The picker shows this clearly, just don't let the number direction trip you up.
- **Points are computed automatically** from Quality, Combat, and whatever Special Rules you've added — you don't need to calculate anything yourself.
- **A gold ✦ next to a member's name** means they've picked up enough Special Rules to be Legend-eligible. It's a suggestion, not automatic — a GM or the player still confirms it with the crown icon in their card.
- **Mark someone Out of Action** during live play if they go down, so the rest of the team (and Command) can see who's still standing.

## General tips for the weekend

- **One device, one role.** If two people share a device, use Switch between turns.
- **Don't panic if something looks a few seconds behind** — it auto-refreshes regularly; give it a moment or nudge a refresh.
- **Report anything broken or confusing to Anthony directly** rather than trying to work around it mid-game — small UI fixes are quick to make between sessions.
- **Costs and thresholds you see (Baraz-Klink prices, special rule costs, god tier thresholds) are still placeholders** in several places — don't read too much into the exact numbers yet, they'll be finalised before the actual event.

Have fun, and may your renown be ever climbing.
